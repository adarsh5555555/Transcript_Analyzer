Interview Guide - European Electric Bus Adoption

Project objective:
Understand city transit adoption, barriers, economics and purchasing behaviour for battery-electric
buses in Europe.

Questions:
1. How would you describe current adoption of electric buses in your market?
2. What are the main barriers to adoption?
3. How important are budgets and total cost of ownership in purchasing decisions?
4. How important are driver training and vehicle reliability?
5. What adoption trend do you expect over the next 3-5 years?
6. What is the typical decision-making timeline for purchasing a new bus fleet?
